GeoTimeline User Guide & Features

Quick reference on how every memory organization feature works.

1 Instant Upload & Auto-Classification

Drag and drop any photos or videos anywhere on the app (or click "Upload Media"). GeoTimeline automatically reads the EXIF timestamp and GPS coordinates, resolving location names and filing your media into [Location] / [YYYY-MM] folders.

2 Chronological Timeline Stream

View your visual history grouped automatically into Year ➔ Month ➔ Day. Use the Time Jump sidebar to jump directly to any year, filter by photo/video type, or export the entire library as an organized ZIP archive.

3 Journeys & Auto/Manual Trips

Click "Auto-Detect Clusters" to cluster travel getaways (>50km from home), or click "+ New Custom Trip" to create a personalized trip with its own uploaded photos, route map, and dates.

4 World Map & Journey Polylines

Explore your spatial footprint on a dark Leaflet map. When you view any trip, GeoTimeline draws a fiery orange dashed journey route connecting your photo waypoints in chronological sequence with numbered milestones.

5 No-Backend Standalone HTML Sharing

Click the share icon on any trip card to download a Single-File Standalone HTML Viewer. You can email this file or open it anywhere offline—it embeds the map and photo viewer with zero server dependencies!

6 Google Drive Cloud Organizer

Switch to Google Drive mode in Storage Settings to automatically back up all organized folder structures to your cloud Drive without consuming local disk space.
